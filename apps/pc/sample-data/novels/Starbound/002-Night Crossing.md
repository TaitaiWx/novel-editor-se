Chapter 2: Night Crossing

Act II

Scene 1 — Midnight

The *Kestrel* slipped out of Port Lumen without a single bell. Mara stood at the bow and watched the city shrink into a handful of sparks.

:::scene{#s-2-1 title="Below the Stars" pov=Mara location="The Kestrel" time=Night}
Somewhere past the breakwater the wind changed. The compass grew warm in her palm, and a thin line of light ran from the needle to the edge of the sea.

"Captain," she whispered. "It's showing the way."

Ives came to stand beside her. For a long moment he said nothing at all.

"Then we follow it," he said. "Wherever it goes."
:::

Scene 2 — The Storm Wall

By dawn the horizon had turned the color of slate. Ahead of them, a wall of cloud stood on the water like a cliff.
