Chapter 1: The Harbor

Act I

Scene 1 — The Pier at Dusk

The harbor of Port Lumen smelled of tar and salt. :char[Mara]{id=Mara Quill} walked the length of the pier with her father's compass in her fist, counting the masts the way other people counted sheep.

"You're late," said a voice from the last berth.

:char[Captain Ives]{id=Captain Ives} did not look up from his charts. A lantern swung above him, painting the black sails gold.

"The tide isn't," Mara said. "Not yet."

Scene 2 — The Bargain

He finally looked at her, then at the compass. The needle was not pointing north. It was pointing at the lighthouse on the horizon, the one nobody had lit in thirty years.

"Where did you get that?"

"It found me," she said. "Will you take me, or do I steal a boat?"

Ives rolled up the chart. "We sail at midnight. Bring a coat. The sea out there doesn't care how brave you are."
