# Starbound · Style Guide

An English companion to 星河旅人, used to show the **English structure rules**:

- Chapters: `Chapter 1: The Harbor`, `Ch. 3`, `Prologue`, `Epilogue`
- Acts: `Act I`, `Act 2`
- Scenes: `Scene 1 — The Pier at Dusk`

Settings → 正文结构 can turn the English preset on or off. The CLI reads the same rules: `ne structure`.

## Voice

- Close third person, past tense.
- Mara Quill: short sentences, dry humour.
- Captain Ives: speaks rarely; never wastes a word.
